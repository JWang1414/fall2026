Notes about the procedure:
- When drawing points to calculate the momentum, I try to stick closer to the collision event. That is, I avoid setting the end of the track far away from the initial collision, even if the particle is stable enough to have traveled far. This is just because particles in the bubble chamber have a tendency to slow down over time. Measuring far from the initial event will yield higher uncertainties, and a momentum smaller than reality
- While I try to keep the angle reference the same between trials, it has to be periodically redrawn so Traxis gets the right angle. Sometimes it will measure an inverted angle
	- For example, providing an angle of 206 degrees, when in reality it is closer to 26 degrees
