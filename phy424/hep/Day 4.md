When measuring the events inside of the bubble chamber, the manual defines the concept of the "invariant mass"
$$
	m^{2} = \left( \sum E_{i} \right)^{2} - \left( \sum \vec{p}_{i} \right)^{2}

The primary strong interactions that occur in the bubble chamber are $\pi^++p$. Both of these particles have a charge $+1$. Inside of the tank, positively charged particles curve downwards. Neutral particles will not show up, so the negative and positive charges will all appear in the bubble chamber.
